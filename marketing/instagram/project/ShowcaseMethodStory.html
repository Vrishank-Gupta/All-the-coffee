The Builder's Method

You don't need to learn coding.

You need 8 questions.

1Spot it. Is this task worth a tool?
2Size it. What's the smallest useful version?
3Choose where. Browser, Python, AI inside, or no tool?
4Brief it. Could a new intern build it from this?
5Know the answer first. What should it say, worked out by hand?
6Build in slices. What's the next small piece?
7Test like a sceptic. How could it be wrong without telling you?
8Before real use. Is it safe for real work?
