Appraisal season

"Handled monthly MIS reporting and supported the team on various initiatives."

That's what most self-reviews say. It's also exactly why they don't get noticed.

If you did any of this this year, say so

Turned a 40-minute weekly report into a 10-minute one

Cleaned up a messy vendor list

Drafted the first version of a policy note

That's appraisal-worthy. Write it like it is.
