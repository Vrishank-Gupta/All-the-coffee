Byte-sized AI fact

AI doesn't read your text letter by letter.

It reads in chunks called tokens.

Words get broken into pieces before the AI ever sees them, so it isn't looking at individual letters the way you are. That's why asking it to count letters, check exact spelling, or do anything character by character can go wrong, even when everything else it says is sharp. For anything like that, ask it to double-check with a tool instead of trusting the first answer.

Small facts. Fewer blind spots.
